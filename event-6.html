📄 About Event

The Mermaid's Waltz — a solo dance competition where movement meets emotion. Grace the stage with your unique style, whether it's classical, bollywood, or freestyle. Let your performance flow like the ocean and captivate the audience.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Time limit — 3–5 minutes
❷ Theme/style — Can be classical, bollywood, or freestyle.
❸ Must be decent and audience appropriate.
❹ Music — should be clean (no abusive or offensive words).
❺ Edited track must be ready before the performance.
❻ Stage coverage — use the whole stage, avoid staying in one spot.
❼ Costume — should suit the dance style.
❽ Avoid overly revealing dresses; keep it comfortable for movement.
❾ Props — are allowed.
