COURT OF THE DEEP

.moot court · debate & advocacy

📄 About Event

Court of the Deep — a prestigious moot court competition where aspiring advocates argue complex legal cases before a panel of esteemed judges. Sharpen your advocacy skills, present compelling arguments, and experience the thrill of the courtroom.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Teams of 2 members per side (prosecution/defense)
❷ Each team will receive the case materials 48 hours in advance
❸ Oral submissions limited to 15 minutes per side
❹ Use of electronic devices during proceedings is prohibited
❺ All participants must dress in formal court attire
❻ Judgement will be based on legal reasoning, presentation, and courtroom etiquette
❼ Any form of plagiarism in written submissions will lead to disqualification
❽ The decision of the judging panel shall be final and binding
