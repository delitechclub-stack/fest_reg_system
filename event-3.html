📄 Event Description

DEBATE COMPETITION – AI & LAW

Organised by: Moot Court Society, De Paul Law College

Debate Topic: “Role of Judiciary in Protecting, Preserving and Promoting Constitutional Objectives and Values in the Era of AI”

The Debate Competition will provide participants with an opportunity to present and defend legal arguments on the given topic. The competition will be conducted in one round, with two participants forming a team. Participants must prepare both FOR and AGAINST the motion, as the side will be allotted by the organisers on the day of the competition. The winner will be decided based on the marks awarded by the judging panel.

⚖️ Judging Criteria
Criteria Marks
Knowledge of Law & Legal Concepts20
Constitutional & Legal Reasoning20
Strength and Relevance of Arguments20
Rebuttal & Counter-Arguments15
Use of Legal Authorities / Cases / Examples10
Clarity, Communication & Presentation10
Time Management & Overall Impact5
Total100
📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Each team shall consist of two participants. Teams may be homogeneous or heterogeneous.
❷ The competition will consist of one round only.
❸ Participants must prepare both FOR and AGAINST the motion. The side will be allotted by the organisers on the day of the competition.
❹ Both team members must actively participate and adhere to the speaking time announced by the organisers.
❺ Arguments must be relevant to the topic and should demonstrate sound legal reasoning, supported by relevant laws, constitutional provisions, case laws, judicial decisions or credible examples wherever appropriate.
❻ Participants must maintain professional decorum. Personal remarks, offensive language, disrespectful comments or attacks against the opposing team are strictly prohibited.
❼ Exceeding the allotted time may result in deduction of marks.
❽ Participants may use notes or references, but must demonstrate independent understanding and legal reasoning.
❾ Plagiarism or deliberate misrepresentation of legal authorities may lead to disqualification. Participants are responsible for the accuracy of information and sources used.
❿ The decision of the judging panel shall be final and binding. The organisers reserve the right to take appropriate decisions on matters not covered by these rules.
