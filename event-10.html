📄 About Event

Poseidon's Cup — a thrilling football tournament for PU students. Teams of 7+3 players will battle it out on the pitch in a fast-paced 10+10 minute format. Show your skills, teamwork, and sportsmanship to claim the cup.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Team Composition: 7 + 3 players · One goalkeeper compulsory · Unlimited rolling substitutions
❷ Match Duration: 10 + 10 minutes · Half-time: 3 min · Extra time: 3 min · Penalty shootout if tied
❸ Gameplay: Kick-off from center · Standard throw-ins · 5-yard distance on free kicks · Standard fouls apply
❹ Discipline: Yellow Card = Official Warning · Red Card = Player sent off (team continues with 6 players)
❺ Winning: Most goals wins · Knockout draws decided by penalty shootout
❻ General Rules: Proper sports attire compulsory · Eligibility forms required · Sportsmanship mandatory · PU students only · Referee's decision final
❼ Reporting: School ID Card & Principal's permission required before tournament commencement
❽ Discipline: Fighting, abuse or serious misconduct may result in suspension or disqualification
❾ Player ID: Every player must carry and wear/display a valid School ID Card and Aadhar card throughout the tournament
❿ Principal's Permission: Every team must submit permission/approval from their Principal or authorized school authority
📋 General Tournament Conditions
📌 The PU Football Tournament Committee may modify the schedule, playing conditions or match arrangements whenever necessary due to venue, weather, safety or administrative reasons.
📌 All teams must cooperate with the Organizing Committee, referees, volunteers and venue authorities and maintain proper discipline and sportsmanship.
📌 During document verification, every participating college/team must produce required documents, valid college ID cards, and documents duly signed and verified by the Principal. Failure to produce documents will result in disqualification. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 Teams must report to the venue on time as per schedule. Late entry or failure to report may result in disqualification or walkover. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 If the fest is extended to a second or subsequent day, the college will provide accommodation only. Food, refreshments and other personal expenses must be arranged by respective teams.
⚠️ NOTE: In case of any unforeseen circumstances or conflicts, the Organizing Committee reserves the right to make the final decision.
