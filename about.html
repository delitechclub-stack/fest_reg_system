About Us

About the College

De Paul College Mysore, founded by the Vincentian fathers with a global presence in the field of education, provides ample space and soaring enthusiasm for inquisitive minds to bloom forth. The healthy ambience of De Paul opens up fresh avenues in which they would rise to spectacular heights and shine. With a sprawling campus spread over 50 acres enveloped by a beautiful landscape and equipped with infrastructure facilities of international standard, De Paul would stimulate and sustain the growth process of the students in its three-fold vital strands: Intellectual, Spiritual and Moral.

About the Fest

Long before empires rose and fell, there was Atlantis — the island kingdom born of Poseidon's own hand, a civilization so magnificent it was said to rival the gods. Conceived by the philosopher Plato over two thousand years ago, its legend has echoed through time — a tale of glory, power, and the sea's eternal secrets. This year, that legend rises again.

The Department of Commerce, Management, Computer Science, Arts and Physical Education proudly presents Deplomat 2K26, an Inter-Collegiate PU/UG Fest that summons the brightest minds to rise from the surface and descend into the lost splendor of Atlantis — City of Poseidon. Legend speaks of a sunken kingdom where knowledge, artistry, and valor once reigned supreme — and this September, that legend is reborn.

Building on the resounding success of Deplomat 2K25, which drew over 1,000 enthusiastic participants, this year's edition rises grander still with 12 thrilling events spanning Core, Cultural, and Sports categories. Every challenge is a gate beneath the waves, and only those with sharp minds, bold hearts, and unshaken spirit shall emerge crowned as the true heirs of Atlantis.

On 25th and 26th September 2026, De Paul College transforms into the sunken city itself — will you answer Poseidon's call and claim your throne beneath the tide?

Principal's Note

Rev. Dr. Baiju Antony CM
Principal, De Paul College, Mysore

Biography: A Vincentian educator and administrator, Rev. Dr. Baiju Antony CM leads De Paul College with a focus on holistic, value-based education. He holds a Ph.D. in Higher Education Leadership, Management and Policy from Seton Hall University, New Jersey, USA, and serves as a national executive member and Karnataka region secretary for the Xavier Board of Higher Education in India. With extensive experience as an administrator, director, and principal across academic institutions, he returned to lead the De Paul campus as principal in May 2021, having previously served in leadership roles here.

Under his leadership, De Paul continues to strengthen its academic and co-curricular ecosystem on its 50-acre, landscaped campus equipped with international-standard infrastructure, nurturing students along intellectual, spiritual, and moral dimensions.
