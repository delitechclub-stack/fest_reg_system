📄 About Event

Tides of Rhythm — a group dance competition where sync and energy take center stage. With 5–12 members per team, showcase your chemistry and creativity across any dance form. Let the rhythm of the ocean guide your moves and captivate the audience.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Team Size: Each team must consist of 5–12 participants.
❷ Dance Forms: Classical, Bollywood, Freestyle, Western, or any other suitable dance form is allowed.
❸ Time Limit: The performance must not exceed 7 minutes.
❹ Music: Songs containing offensive, vulgar, abusive, or inappropriate words/content are strictly prohibited.
❺ Costume: Costumes must be decent and appropriate for a college event.
❻ Props: Props are permitted, provided they are safe and do not damage the stage.
❼ Content: Performances must not contain obscene, discriminatory, or offensive gestures/actions.
❽ Audio Submission: The final performance track must be submitted to the organizers before the deadline.
❾ Reporting Time: Teams must report to the venue 30 minutes before their performance.
❿ Judges' Decision: The decision of the judges will be final and binding.
