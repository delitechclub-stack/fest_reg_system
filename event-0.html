📄 About Event

THE ATLANTEAN TRIALS is an immersive Escape Room experience designed to push your teamwork, observation, coordination, logic and problem-solving to the limit. With 5 distinct trials to conquer, your team of 3 must navigate through puzzles, challenges and cryptic clues — all against the clock. Only the sharpest minds will emerge victorious. 📋 Rules & Regulations
1 Team Size — Each team must consist of 3 participants only.
2 5 Trials — The event consists of five different trials testing teamwork, observation, coordination, logic and problem-solving.
3 Time & Sequence — All trials must be completed within the given time limit and in the specified sequence. No skipping allowed.
4 No External Help — Mobile phones, internet searches and external assistance are strictly prohibited.
5 Active Participation — Both team members must actively participate in the challenges.
6 Respect Props — Participants must not damage or tamper with any props, puzzles or equipment.
7 No Cheating — Cheating, external help or disturbing other teams is strictly prohibited.
8 Follow Instructions — Participants must follow the instructions given by the organizers and volunteers at all times.
9 Winner — The team completing the final challenge in the shortest time will be declared the winner.
10 Final Decision — The decision of the event coordinators will be final and binding.
