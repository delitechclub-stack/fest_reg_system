📄 About Event

The Lost Relic — a thrilling treasure hunt across the campus. Teams of two will decipher cryptic clues, solve puzzles, and race against time to uncover the hidden relic. Stay sharp, work together, and claim the glory.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Teams must follow the clues in the given order. Skipping any clue is not permitted.
❷ Mobile phones and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited during the event.
❸ Participants must not tamper with, hide, or interfere with clues intended for other teams.
❹ Entry into restricted or unauthorized areas is strictly prohibited.
❺ Cheating, unfair practices, or any damage to college property will result in immediate disqualification.
❻ All participants must strictly adhere to the instructions given by the event coordinators. The decision of the event coordinators shall be final and binding.
