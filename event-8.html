📄 About Event

Runway of Realms — a grand ramp walk competition where style meets storytelling. Teams of 10–12 members will showcase their theme through stunning outfits, choreography, and stage presence. Walk the runway and bring your realm to life.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Team Size: Each team must consist of 10–12 participants.
❷ Theme: Teams may either follow the given Atlantis theme or choose a completely different Open Theme of their own.
❸ Performance Duration: The performance must be between 7–8 minutes. Music and all activities must stop after 8 minutes.
❹ Performance: Teams may use group formations, coordinated solos, or a combination of both.
❺ Costume: Outfits must be decent, appropriate, and suitable for a college event. Vulgar, overly revealing, or inappropriate dressing is strictly prohibited.
❻ Props: Props are permitted, provided they are safe and do not cause any damage to the stage, venue, or college property. Dangerous, sharp, flammable, or harmful props are strictly prohibited.
❼ Theme Presentation: Outfits, styling, props, and performance should complement and represent the team's chosen theme.
❽ Question & Answer Round: Each team will have a Q&A round based on their chosen theme, where they should explain their theme, why they chose it, and how their outfits and performance represent it.
❾ Audio Submission: The final performance track must be submitted to the organizers before the deadline.
❿ Reporting Time: Teams must report to the venue and be ready before their scheduled performance time.
⓫ Discipline: Participants must maintain proper decorum and respect the venue, coordinators, judges, and other teams. Unnecessary noise, running, fighting, or misconduct will not be tolerated.
⓬ Assigned Room: One room will be assigned to each team and must be kept clean and maintained properly.
⓭ Content: Performances must not contain offensive, disrespectful, obscene, or inappropriate content.
⓮ Judges' Decision: The decision of the judges and event coordinators will be final and binding.
