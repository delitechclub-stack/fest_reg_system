📄 About Event

The Kraken's Pitch — a thrilling box-cricket tournament for PU & UG Boys. Teams of 7+3 players will battle it out in a fast-paced format. Show your batting, bowling, and fielding skills to claim the crown in this high-energy competition.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Eligibility & Team: Only bona fide UG & PU students · 7+3 players · One player per team only · Team changes subject to committee approval
❷ Mandatory Documents: Valid College/School ID · Aadhaar Card · Participation Letter from Principal · No letter = No entry · Verification at any stage
❸ Match Format: League: 5 overs per side · Semi-Final & Final: 7 overs per side · 30-yards box-cricket conditions
❹ Bowling Rules (5-Over): 1 over each by 4 bowlers · Remaining over assigned as permitted · (7-Over): 2-2-1-1-1 pattern · Bowling Powerplay: Over 1 mandatory
❺ Batting Powerplay: May be taken between Over 2 and final over · Must be communicated before over starts · Cannot be shifted once over begins
❻ Field Restrictions & Decisions: Enforced as directed by umpire · All fielders within permitted area during powerplay · Umpire's decision is FINAL
❼ Reporting & Dress Code: Report on time as per schedule · Late reporting may result in walkover · Proper sports kit & sports shoes compulsory
❽ Discipline – Zero Tolerance: No alcohol, drugs, violence, abuse, cheating, match-fixing, forged documents, or property damage · Maintain sportsmanship · Serious misconduct = immediate removal
❾ Grounds for Disqualification: Ineligible player · Forged documents · Violence or misconduct · Cheating or match-fixing · Repeated rule violations · Committee decision final
📋 General Tournament Conditions
📌 The Cricket Tournament Committee may modify the schedule, playing conditions, or match arrangements due to venue, weather, safety, or administrative reasons.
📌 All teams must cooperate with Organizing Committee, referees, volunteers, and venue authorities, maintaining proper discipline and sportsmanship.
📌 Document Verification: Every college must produce required documents, valid college ID cards, and documents signed by Principal. Failure results in disqualification. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 Teams must report to venue on time as per schedule. Late entry may result in disqualification or walkover. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 If the fest is extended to a second or subsequent day, the college will provide accommodation only. Food, refreshments, and other personal expenses must be arranged by teams.
⚠️ NOTE: In case of any unforeseen circumstances or conflicts, the Organizing Committee reserves the right to modify or amend the rules accordingly.
