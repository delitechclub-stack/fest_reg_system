📄 About Event

Riptide Rally — an electrifying throwball tournament exclusively for Undergraduate (UG) Girls. Teams of 9+3 players will compete in a best-of-three sets format. Spike, set, and dive your way to victory in a high‑energy competition that celebrates teamwork, agility, and sportsmanship.

📋 Rules & Regulations
❶ Objective: Throw the ball over the net to land inside opponent's court while preventing them from doing the same.
❷ Starting: Match begins with a toss. Winner chooses to serve first or select the side of the court.
❸ Service: Made from behind the end line. Ball must be thrown over the net without stepping on or crossing the service line.
❹ Catching: Player may catch with both hands. Ball must be returned promptly without unnecessary holding.
❺ Throwing: Ball must be thrown toward opponent's court. Kicking, punching, or striking with other body parts is prohibited.
❻ Scoring: Point awarded when ball lands in opponent's court, opponent throws out, fails to return, or commits a fault.
❼ Ball Outside: If ball lands completely outside opponent's court, opposing team receives the point.
❽ Net Rules: Players must not touch the net during play. Deliberate interference with net or opponent is a fault.
❾ Court Boundaries: Players must observe marked boundaries and service/playing-area restrictions.
❿ Substitution: May be made according to tournament regulations through the appropriate official.
⓫ Conduct: Respect referees, officials, teammates, and opponents. Abusive language, deliberate physical contact, and unsporting conduct are prohibited.
⓬ Match Format: Best of three sets · Each set played to 15 points · Third set if tied.
⓭ Time-outs: Allowed according to tournament regulations, requested by captain or coach through official.
⓮ Team Composition: 12 players total · 9 on court · Remaining as substitutes · Designated captain required.
⓯ Uniform: Team jersey with visible numbers · Matching shorts · Suitable sports shoes and socks.
⓰ Eligibility: Proper registration · Valid college ID · Represent only registered institution · Follow referee instructions.
📋 General Tournament Conditions
📌 The Throwball Tournament Committee may modify schedule, playing conditions, or match arrangements due to venue, weather, safety, or administrative reasons.
📌 All teams must cooperate with Organizing Committee, referees, volunteers, and venue authorities, maintaining proper discipline and sportsmanship.
📌 Document Verification: Every college must produce required documents, valid college ID cards, and documents signed by Principal. Failure results in disqualification. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 Teams must report to venue on time as per schedule. Late entry may result in disqualification or walkover. Registration fee is non-refundable.
📌 If the fest is extended to a second or subsequent day, the college will provide accommodation only. Food, refreshments, and other personal expenses must be arranged by teams.
⚠️ NOTE: In case of any unforeseen circumstances or conflicts, the Organizing Committee reserves the right to modify or amend the rules accordingly.
